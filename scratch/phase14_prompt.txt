============================================================
1. PRODUCT
============================================================

Product name:

OraVisionAI

OraVisionAI is an AI-assisted oral-health screening and teleconsultation platform.

The system combines:

- AI-based oral condition classification
- YOLO-based lesion detection
- Explainable AI
- Patient medical profiles
- Screening history
- AI-generated results
- Dentist review
- Dentist-patient communication
- Appointment management
- Teleconsultation
- Administrator management
- Secure authentication
- Structured medical data storage


============================================================
2. PRIMARY USER ROLES
============================================================

There are three primary roles:

1. PATIENT
2. DENTIST
3. ADMINISTRATOR


============================================================
3. PATIENT WORKFLOW
============================================================

PUBLIC LANDING PAGE
-------------------

When a visitor first enters OraVisionAI, they should see a professional full-screen landing experience explaining:

- What OraVisionAI is
- What the platform does
- AI-assisted oral screening
- Dentist consultation
- Patient benefits
- How the process works
- Appropriate calls to action

The website must look like a professionally designed medical/dental technology product.

It must NOT look like an automatically generated generic dashboard.

The landing page should include:

- Login
- Sign Up

Use polished animations and transitions.


AUTHENTICATION
--------------

Patients must be able to authenticate using:

- Email/username + password
- Google Login through Firebase Authentication

Authentication must be handled securely.

The frontend must never be trusted to determine authorization.

The backend must verify the authenticated Firebase identity.


PATIENT ONBOARDING
------------------

After registration, the patient should complete a medical profile.

The profile should include appropriate patient information such as:

- Name
- Age/date of birth
- Gender where appropriate
- Contact information
- Medical background
- Medical history
- Allergies
- Current medications
- Previous dental/oral health history
- Relevant additional information

Do not collect unnecessary personal information.

The medical profile should be editable later.


PATIENT DASHBOARD
-----------------

The patient dashboard should provide access to:

- Profile
- Medical profile
- New screening
- Screening history
- AI results
- Reports
- XAI explanations
- Dentist consultation
- Appointments
- Messages
- Notifications
- Account settings


SCREENING
---------

Patient can upload an oral/dental image.

The uploaded image must be stored securely.

The backend should create a screening record.

The screening pipeline is:

Patient image
    ↓
Secure storage
    ↓
FastAPI
    ↓
OraVisionAI YOLO detector
    ↓
OraVisionAI EfficientNet classifier
    ↓
Risk assessment
    ↓
XAI
    ↓
Report generation
    ↓
Patient result


AI RESULTS
----------

Patients can see their AI result after analysis.

Results should clearly distinguish:

- AI-generated prediction
- Confidence
- Detected regions
- Risk assessment
- Explainability

The system must NOT represent an AI prediction as a definitive medical diagnosis.

The interface should communicate that AI results are assistive/informational and that professional evaluation may be required.


SCREENING HISTORY
-----------------

Patients can view previous screening records.

Each screening should contain:

- Image
- Date/time
- AI classification
- Confidence
- YOLO detections
- Risk result
- XAI visualizations
- Report
- Dentist assessment when available


SCREENING DELETION
------------------

Patients may delete their own screening records.

Use appropriate soft-deletion behavior at the application level.

A patient must never be able to delete another patient's record.


DENTIST CONTACT
---------------

Patients can contact dentists.

Patients may:

- Browse available dentists
- View relevant dentist profile information
- Send messages
- Request/book consultation
- View appointment status


TELECONSULTATION
----------------

The system will use Stream for communication/teleconsultation functionality.

Depending on the final implementation:

- Video call
- Audio call
- Messaging

should be supported.

Appointments and actual consultation sessions are separate concepts.

The backend should create/manage the Stream consultation session securely.

Patients should only access consultations they are authorized to join.


============================================================
4. DENTIST WORKFLOW
============================================================

DENTIST REGISTRATION
--------------------

Dentists can register through the platform.

Dentists provide professional information including appropriate fields such as:

- Name
- Contact information
- Professional credentials
- License information
- Specialization
- Experience
- Professional bio
- Verification documentation where required


DENTIST VERIFICATION
--------------------

Dentists may initially have:

PENDING

verification status.

Administrators can:

- Review dentist information
- Review verification documents
- Approve dentist
- Reject dentist
- Suspend dentist where appropriate

Only appropriately verified dentists should become available for patient consultations.


DENTIST DASHBOARD
-----------------

Dentists should have access to:

- Dashboard
- Patient management
- Patient list
- Patient profiles
- Screening history
- AI results
- XAI reports
- Dentist assessments
- Appointments
- Availability
- Messages
- Teleconsultations
- Notifications
- Professional profile


PATIENT MANAGEMENT
------------------

Dentists can manage patients associated with them.

A dentist should NOT automatically have unrestricted access to every patient's private medical data.

Access should follow the patient-dentist relationship and authorization rules.

Dentists can view relevant patient screening history.

Dentists can review screenings generated before and after consultations.


DENTIST ASSESSMENT
------------------

Dentists can provide their own professional assessment associated with a screening.

This may contain:

- Clinical notes
- Recommendations
- Professional observations

The dentist assessment must remain separate from the AI prediction.

IMPORTANT:

AI RESULT != DENTIST ASSESSMENT


APPOINTMENTS
------------

Dentists should be able to:

- Define availability
- Accept/reject requests
- View upcoming appointments
- View past appointments
- Cancel appointments where appropriate
- Conduct completed consultations


MESSAGING
---------

Dentists can communicate with authorized patients.

Messaging should be associated with a patient-dentist relationship.

Do not expose conversations to unrelated users.
